  • HOLI

    Saraff Global supported Holi festival 2013 organized at Shri Vishnu Temple,Bangkok.The festival was celebrated on 3 different days.The holika dahan was held on 26 March.The fun filled event hasya holi hangama was on 30March in Holiday Inn.The last event was holi rangotsav on 31March. Organizers are provided free bunch and chance to celebrate holi with water and organic colors.

  • DIWALI

    Saraff Global is proud to sponsor events like Diwali celebration.The celebration was on 26 October 2013 at Indian Thai Chamber of Commerce. The celebration was on a beautiful evening with colorful & vibrant cultural shows. The event was organized to unite Indian Community in Thailand to celebrate the festival with lots of joy and happiness.

  • PLANTATION

    Saraff Global supported "Plant a Tree" program.The company invited its employees and everyone to join the tree evolution. We pride ourselves in making green living fun,sharing valuable education on trees,monitoring our trees and bringing people together to change environment eco-friendly.

  • INDEPENDENCE DAY

    Independence day of India was celebrated on 15 august 2014 in Bangkok with traditional enthusiasm.Ambassdor Harsh Vardhan Shringla unfurled the national tricolor at the Embassy premises. National Anthem and patriotic songs were sung.The Ambassador read out the President's Address to the Nation. Various Indian associations collaborated to put up a cultural performance on the occasion. Around 500 people including members of the Indian community and the Embassy of India together with Thai nationals attended the function.

  • FUN FAIR IN THAILAND

    Saraff Global supported the first "Thai-Indian Fun Fair" to boost the friendship in the Thai-Indian community held at Srinakharinwirot University on Sukhumvit Soi 23 into a social cultural platform where 3000 members of the Thai-Indian community where gathered for a fun evening.Filled with enrichment experiences of all ages, the fair presented a unique cultural activities.Stalls and booths featured specific interests and unique experiences such as health spas,jewelry,games,music and entertainment.

  • WORLD SANSKRIT CONFERENCE

    Saraff Global supported 15th World Sanskrit Conference at the Sanskrit Study Centre,Silpakorn University in Bangkok. The conference was organized from July in honor of Princess Maha Chakri Sirindhorn's.
