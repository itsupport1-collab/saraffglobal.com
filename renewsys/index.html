Renewsys

RENEWSYS CO., LTD. is a full service distributor of industrial air pollution control systems and products. We see environmental engineering as a key component of industrialization. A means of ensuring a cleaner and better tomorrow.

Air pollution control division can fulfill the entire air pollution control requirements from a single source – designing, sizing, engineering, manufacturing, installation and commissioning of dry and wet type of pollution control systems, together with auxiliary equipments.

Our designs are based on know-how from:

  • ESC Umwelttechnik, Gmbh, Germany for Electrostatic Precipitators.
  • Beltran Technologies, USA for Wet Electrostatic Precipitators.
  • Wheelaborator, USA for Bag Filters.

Industrial fans division supplies heavy duty centrifugal fans of large, medium and small capacities which are designed to withstand heavy dust load, high temperature and high pressure conditions for various applications. These fans are compact, robust and are tailor made for specific requirements. With technical know-how from Venti-Oelde GmbH, Germany it has catered to needs of many customers for their various applications. No wonder they are in great demand in industries such as steel, cement, sugar, boiler manufacturers, power stations, chemicals and petro-chemicals.
