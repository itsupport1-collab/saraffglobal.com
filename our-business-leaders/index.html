Susheel Kumar Saraff

Chairman/Founder

  • Susheel is the founder and visionary Chairman of Saraff Group of Companies.
  • 40 years of experience as a successful entrepreneur.
  • Under his dynamic, exemplary leadership, Saraff Group is now a diversified conglomerate with business presence in US, Thailand, India, and Hong Kong.
  • Popularly known as "BOSS" by all the Saraff employees, Mr. Saraff is a very friendly and interactive person who encourages and supports employees in their work.

Social Activities:

  • Mr. Saraff was awarded with "Pravasi Bharatiya Samman 2017", the highest civilian honour conferred on overseas Indians conferred on Susheel Kumar Saraff. Recognized for his work in business, society and advancing human consciousness, now investing in start-ups. Hon'ble Pranab Mukherjee, President of India presented the award on 9th Jan.
  • As the President of the India-Thai Chamber of Commerce (April 2011-April 2015), he has provided strategic leadership to the Board of Directors and established the Chamber to new heights by creating business opportunities for its members.
  • Mr. Saraff is also serving as International Working President for International Vaish Federation and President of VHP Association Thailand. He is one of the Trustees of Vishwa Hindu Parishad.
  • He also runs the Shri Dadiji Mandir Trust, Titlagarh, Odisha that organize annual free eye cataract surgery camp for the people living in remote areas.
  • Under the patronage of the Embassy of India, Mr. Saraff is a founder member and Convener of Indian Social Club of Thailand that represents about 65 Associations of Indian Community in Thailand.
