SARAFF ENTERPRISES Co., Ltd

27 May 2025

Saraff Enterprises started it’s journey way back in 1987, in the diamond business, pioneering the trade in Bangkok. Since then, in last two decades, Saraff has created a name of it’s own in the tradition of quality and excellence. Saraff Enterprises specializes in diamond of large sizes and meeting customer demands globally. With it’s office in Mumbai and Hong Kong. Saraff Enterprises has strengthened it’s foothold in the global market.

VISION:

Trusted global provider for a variety of diamonds and premium custom made jewelry at best value.

MISSION:

Build capabilities in better servicing the clients and accelerate mutual growth.
