OUR HISTORY

  • 1987

    Saraff Enterprises started it's journey way back in 1987, in the diamond business,pioneering the trade in Bangkok.Since then,in last 28 years, Saraff has created a name of it's own in the tradition of quality and excellence. Saraff Enterprises specializes in diamond of large sizes and meeting customer demands globally. With it's office in Belgium,Saraff Enterprises has strengthened it's foothold in the Europe market.

  • 1989

  • 1994

    Sinter Plast Thai Ltd. was established in 1994 as a joint venture between M / s KP Associates a company under the Saraff Group and M / s Sintex International Ltd. of India under promotion of Board of Investment Thailand.

  • 2004

    Since 2004, Saraff Infotech has earned a reputation as a unique firm in a fast-paced marketplace, gathering momentum in each passing year. Focused on people, process, and technology, we ensure that our customers capture the benefits of any solutions implemented to maximize the return on our IT investment.

  • 2005

    Saraff Holdings is a part of the Saraff Group, established in 2005 with a vision to develop and populate the Rama III area in Bangkok into a comfortable commercial and residential space. Bangkok's property market boasts some of the most magnificent, most imaginative residences out there. Not just in Thailand or Southeast Asia, but the world over.

  • 2009

    SEL is geared towards growing in the Energy sector and aims to achieve 200 MW of generating capacity by the year 2009. SEL will develop a strong reputation for quality of work and a healthy respect for sustaining the environment. SEL is committed to its mission of clean energy and would look to develop innovative ways to achieve the same.
