 Saraff Ventures Growing Tomorrow’s IdeasSaraff Ventures was built on the Saraff Group’s ethos of business excellence with social significance by leveraging eco-friendly technologies and human capital. Incorporated in September 2014 in Singapore with the aim of proliferating the Group’s entrepreneurial spirit, Saraff Ventures focuses on investments related to healthcare, fintech, venture funds, consumer focussed technology startups, and online brands. Saraff Ventures Pte. Ltd. currently manages a portfolio of 8+ companies, with investments exceeding 1 Million USD.
