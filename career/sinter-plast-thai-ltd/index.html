RENEWSYS CO ., LTD

The Saraff Plastics industry is into manufacturing Fresh Water Tanks and Moulded furnitures.We manufactures fresh water tanks ranging from 100 to 11,000 litres which covers Overhead Tanks, Septic Tanks, Underground Tanks and many more. These products are known all over Thailand under the name of ' TRIDENT '.
VISION:
To innovate, excel and expand in Plastics industry by leveraging our brands Trident
MISSION:
• To achieve growth through innovation in business model and marketing by offering a wide range of high quality plastic products for different industries.
• Build capabilities in better servicing the clients and accelerate mutual growth
