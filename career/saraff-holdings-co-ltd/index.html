SARAFF HOLDINGS Co., Ltd

30 October 2015
Saraff Holdings is a part of the Saraff Group, established in 2005 with a vision to develop and populate the Rama III area in Bangkok into a comfortable commercial and residential space. Several other condominiums and office spaces are available in the most desirable and exclusive locations.Driven by a passion for excellence, luxury and determination to deliver rental or sales properties to its customers as quickly as possible, the company is working with real estate agents, corporate and individuals for cost effective packages. Saraff Holdings manage all their properties , the group’s diverse range of expertise allows them to control property purchase activity, appointment of agents, custom design them for specific clients. Saraff holdings provides commercial and residential space in Bangkok and other cities in Thailand.
VISION:
To invest in and manage properties with long-term value and appreciation
MISSION:
Provide memorable experience through a high-touch and high-tech real estate Company.
