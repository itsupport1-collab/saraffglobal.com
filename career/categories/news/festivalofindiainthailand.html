Fun Fair in Thailand

The Thai-Indian Fun Fair was an event organised by the Indian Social Club of Thailand to bring together the Thai and Indian communities living in Thailand. Saraff Group has been an important part of organising this event since it was started in 2014. We at Saraff Group sponsor this event and participate in organizing it effectively.
