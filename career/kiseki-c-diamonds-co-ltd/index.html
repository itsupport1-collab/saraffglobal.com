KISEKII C DIAMONDS CO., LTD. Saraff Global has been in the diamond trading industry since its inception in 1987, With its 40 years of experience in Diamond trading, in 2022 they looked into setting up Lab-grown diamond industry which would be first of its kind in Thailand. Lab-Grown diamond or “Green Diamond” are as real as diamonds mined from the earth. Lab grown diamonds are identical to earth mined diamonds in every way, except that they are grown in a lab. The trial production will start from April 2023 and full scale commercial production to be online by July 2023.
