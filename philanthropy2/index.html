Philanthropy

Our Beliefs

Saraff Group firmly believes that opportunities given by the almighty to those successful in life’s definitions of achievements have a morale responsibility to contribute towards the betterment of the society. This belief is also manifested in the thoughts that irrespective of the religion, caste and creed, the real spirit of the humanistic values of the holy teachings of Gods, Saints and ancient scriptures needs to be spread in its true objectives, i.e. to create harmony, peace and progress of the society and people.

Saraff Group’s philanthropical efforts are intended to :

Uphold the sanctity of Karma: by providing a helping hand to those underprivileged people who lacked, not the desire to live better lives, not the will and ability to work had but the opportunities to make a difference to their lives.

The Hindu teachings accept the concept of reincarnation (the next existence), and what determines the fate of an individual in the next existence is Karma which refers to the actions undertaken by the body and the mind. The Foundation strives to assist achievement of dreams by providing resources & assistance for a combination of emotional, physical and academic programs designed to enhance quality of life of the underprivileged who may be deprived of basic options and right to knowledge in life. The educational initiatives assist in fuelling a better build up of children’s character, self-esteem and enhancement of career possibilities by providing sound education through contemporary knowledge.

Recognize the practical relevance of Dharma:by helping people truly understand the human values of camaraderie which can be enjoyed by honest compassion to achieve greater personal and professional success in life.

In order to achieve good Karma, it is important to live life according to Dharma – what is right. This involves doing what is right for the individual, the family, the class or caste, the community, the society, for the whole universe itself. Dharma implies the “law of being” without which things cannot exist, duty, a course of conduct. The Foundation relentlessly supports the efforts of the selfless, enlightened souls who are walking on earth to spread the light of true knowledge through religious discourses, socially beneficial initiatives, communications intended to guide stressed and distressed minds and guidance through religious events to restore the fast reducing faith in God and time tested values of human life.
