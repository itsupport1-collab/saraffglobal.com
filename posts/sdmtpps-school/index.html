Shree Dadiji Mandir Trust Prabhavathi Public School

10 July 2015 · Philanthropy

Shree Dadiji Mandir Trust Prabhavathi Public School has been started with an objective to impart quality education to the pupils of educationally and economically backward KBK region at an affordable cost. Sri Susheel Saraff, the Chairperson of the trust has encouraged the school to process every year by leaps and bounds.
