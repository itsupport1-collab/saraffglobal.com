International Yoga Day

10 July 2015 · Philanthropy

About 7500 people joined the First International Day of Yoga in Bangkok. The mass event showcased the yoga protocol demonstration for peace and harmony. Congratulations to the Embassy of India in Bangkok for organizing such as wonderful event under the leadership of H.E. Shri Harsh V Shringla, Ambassador of India to Thailand. Saraff group was one of the main sponsors for this event.
