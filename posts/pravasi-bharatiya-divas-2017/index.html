Pravasi Bharatiya Divas 2017

1 March 2017

Pravasi Bharatiya Divas 2017*

Saturday, January 07 to Monday, January 09, 2017 at Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

*Special Notes*

*Registrations Open till 7 Jan at www.pbdIndia.gov.in*

4000 Delegates from 70 countries already registered.

Special registration fees for Groups and Young Overseas Indians.

*PBD: A Unique Platform for Overseas Indians*

-Opportunity to meet Chief Ministers, Policy Makers,Entrepreneurs, Stakeholders, Corporate Sector

-Explore Business/Investment opportunities

-Delegates can pre book B2B & B2G meetings online at dedicated business platform www.pbdb2b.in

-Tap opportunities and platforms for 'Giving back to India' through IDF-OI

*Highlights of PBD Convention*

*Chief Guest*:

-His Excellency Dr. Antonio Costa, Prime Minister of Portugal

-Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi's address at the inaugural session

-President of India Shri Pranab Mukherjee confers Pravasi Bharatiya Samman Awards (PBSA) 2017 to 30 Outstanding NRIs/PIOs

-Address by Chief Guest Youth PBD H.E Mr. Michael Ashwin Ashim, Vice President, Suriname

-Chief Ministers' participation.

*Exhibition*

-Flagship programmes of Govt of India

-Opportunities in States for NRIs & PIOs

-Schemes & programmes for NRIs & PIOs

-Corporate India, Public Sector Undertakings

-Indian Craft & Culture Bazaar

*Industrial Visits (by Karnataka Udyog Mitra) on 7 & 10 January*

-International Tech Park Ltd. & Export Promotion Industrial Park, Whitefield

-NASSCOM Start-up Warehouse, IAMAI Mobile 10x Accelerator, Centre for Excellence for IOT, Domlur

-Bio- Innovation Centre & Infosys, Electronics City

-Aerospace & Precision component manufacturing industries in Hitech, Defence & Aerospace Park
